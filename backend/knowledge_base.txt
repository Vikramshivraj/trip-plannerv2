AI Travel Planner - Official Knowledge Base & Policies

1. Refund & Cancellation Policy
- Trips cancelled more than 7 days before the start date receive a 100% full refund.
- Trips cancelled between 3 and 7 days receive a 50% refund.
- Trips cancelled less than 3 days before the start date are non-refundable.
- To process a refund, users must email refunds@aitravelplanner.com with their Trip ID.

2. Supported Payment Methods
- We currently accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and UPI.
- We do NOT accept cryptocurrency, PayPal, or Cash on Delivery.
- All payments are secured via bank-grade SSL encryption.

3. Airline Baggage Guidelines (General)
- Cabin Baggage: Most domestic flights allow 1 piece up to 7 kg (dimensions 55cm x 35cm x 25cm).
- Check-in Baggage: Standard economy domestic flights allow 1 piece up to 15 kg. 
- Excess baggage is usually charged at ?500 per additional kg at the airport.

4. Emergency Support
- Our 24/7 travel emergency helpline is +91-1800-555-0199.
- For non-emergencies, please use the support chat or email support@aitravelplanner.com.

5. Visa Assistance
- We currently provide automated Visa checklists for 50+ countries.
- We do not apply for the visa on your behalf. You must submit the application to the respective embassy.
